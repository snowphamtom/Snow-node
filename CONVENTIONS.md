# NINE CIRCLES — Locked compositional conventions

Fixed in writing during the 2026-10-09 authoring sessions and held across the
series. These are the author's rules, not post-hoc description.

1. **Tier hierarchy:** yellow/gold center is the highest tier and master scale.
2. **Entryways:** both side entryways red; overlaps transition through orange.
3. **Composition:** vesica-piscis — each image a circle, circles overlapping with
   color transitions at the intersections. No hard internal frames.
4. **Center treatment:** the black ring around the gold center is removed.
5. **Edge opacity:** yellow layer edges at 50% opacity.
6. **Chirality:** all spirals rotate the same way; golden-ratio quad rotation,
   yellow as master scale.
7. **Blue's role:** blue is axis shadow, not a primary entryway.
8. **Source fidelity:** when the author names an image, that image is mapped onto
   the structure, not reinterpreted.
9. **Background constancy:** background, light, temperature, and sound stay
   constant as the base layer; character and detail layer over it.
