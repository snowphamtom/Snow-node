## What "directed" means here (verifiable behaviors)

1. Reference-driven synthesis: the author supplied reference imagery per circle.
2. Rejection kills branches: directions were rejected outright and retained as
   scrapped (REJECTED.md), not silently re-rolled.
3. Convention locks: compositional rules were fixed in writing during the sessions
   and held across pieces (CONVENTIONS.md).
4. Source fidelity: when the author named an image, that image was mapped onto the
   structure, not reinterpreted.
